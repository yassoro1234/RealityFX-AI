# RealityFX AI – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-03

## Summary

RealityFX AI works entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, or share personal data.

## What the app does and does not do

- **No permissions.** The app requests no Android permissions. It cannot access the internet, your contacts, files, camera, microphone, location, or screen content.
- **No screen capture.** The app never records or reads the screen of other apps or games.
- **No analytics, ads, or third-party SDKs.** The app includes no tracking libraries.
- **Local storage only.** Your settings, game profiles, language, theme, and performance mode are saved in the app's private storage on your phone using Android SharedPreferences. They never leave the device.
- **Sample image.** The before/after preview uses a scene that the app draws itself. It does not use your photos.

## Data you control

- To erase all stored data, open Android Settings, choose Apps, select RealityFX AI, then Storage, then Clear data. Uninstalling the app also removes its data.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in the app's source code and release notes.

Use the contact details given on the app's store listing or in the project repository.
